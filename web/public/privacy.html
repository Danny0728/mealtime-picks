Privacy

Mealtime Picks · last updated 2 November 2026

Mealtime Picks suggests films, series and shows to watch. You can use it without an account; signing in with Google is optional and only used to keep your picks the same across your devices.

Without signing in

Everything you set or save (your platforms, languages, picks, ratings, watchlist) stays in your browser on your device. Nothing about you is sent to us.

If you sign in with Google

  • From your Google account we receive your name, email address and profile picture, through Google Firebase Authentication. They are used only to sign you in and to show who is signed in.
  • Your app data (settings, picks, ratings, watchlist) is stored in Google Cloud Firestore under your account, so your devices stay in sync. Only you can read or change it.
  • For the Popular list, the titles you rated (which ones you loved, and which you rated otherwise) are stored as title ids under an anonymous account id. Anyone using the app can read these ids to count what's popular; they don't include your name or email.

What we don't do

  • No ads, no analytics or tracking scripts.
  • We don't sell or share your information with anyone.

Services the app uses

  • Google Firebase (sign-in and storage): Google Privacy Policy
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app: GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Posters come from TMDB and YouTube, and fonts from Google Fonts; your browser loads them directly from those services.

Deleting your data

Sign out and clear the site's data in your browser to remove what's on your device. To delete what's stored with your account, email us and we'll remove it.
